## Scope

The executed comparison described in the article is LightGBM versus Student-t GARCH(1,1) and naïve persistence. Its target is five-observation forward sample volatility from daily log returns. Its proposed decision layer defines exposure and risk-budget adherence; business outcomes have not been computed in the article.

The final article explicitly excludes executed LSTM/Transformer comparisons, feature-group ablations, SHAP attribution, and regime-conditioned results. Those topics appear in older materials or optional historical code and are not presented as evidence for this paper.
